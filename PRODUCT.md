## Users

CardBranch serves two overlapping groups. Freelancers and solopreneurs who want a polished digital business card they can share via QR code or link — no app download, no design skills. Companies and team leads who need branded cards in bulk for their sales teams, events, or offices. Both groups share a need for something that feels premium without being fussy, and both interact with the product across two surfaces: a public marketing site (landing, sign-up, links page) and an authenticated dashboard (card creation, ordering, admin).

## Product Purpose

CardBranch lets anyone create a premium digital business card with a smart links page, QR code, and print-ready PDF in minutes. Users can then order physical card prints — in tiers from a basic sample pack up to a premium bulk run — delivered to their door. The product exists to make a business card feel like a deliberate choice again, not an afterthought. Success means a user who lands on the marketing page trusts the product enough to sign up, creates their first card in under 5 minutes, and converts to a paid order.

## Brand Personality

Professional, trustworthy, refined. The voice is direct and understated — no superlatives, no hype. It communicates confidence through precision and restraint, not volume.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS / startup monoculture.** No cookie-cutter indigo-and-white dashboards, no stock illustration sets, no templated hero sections. The product should not look like a clone of every other SaaS tool.
- **Cheap / DIY.** Nothing about the experience should suggest a weekend project. That means consistent spacing, intentional typography, no rough edges, no placeholder design.
- **Dark / gamer aesthetic.** The palette is dark but warm and sophisticated — not the blue-black cyberpunk dark of gaming UIs. No neon, no glow effects, no aggressive saturation.

## Design Principles

1. **Premium without pretense.** High-end feel without flash. Quality lives in the details — spacing, alignment, type — not in decoration or ornament.
2. **Confidence through clarity.** Every screen makes the next action obvious. The interface is quiet; the user's work is loud.
3. **Both sides of the glass.** The brand surface (marketing site) and the product surface (dashboard) share a design DNA but serve different jobs. Consistent identity, distinct treatments.
4. **Trust through craft.** A tool for business cards must itself inspire trust. Deliberate micro-copy, reliable interactions, no broken states.
5. **Accessibility is not optional.** WCAG AA as a baseline — contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader support — baked in, not bolted on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text. Full keyboard navigation. Screen reader compatible markup. Reduced motion respected.
